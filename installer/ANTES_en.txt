WHAT YOU NEED (ALL FROM YOUR OWN CONSOLE)

  [required]     prod.keys - your console keys
  [required]     ASTRAL CHAIN base (.nsp or .xci, version 1.0.0) - dumped by you
  [required]     Update 1.0.1 (.nsp) - dumped by you
  [recommended]  Your console firmware (.zip or folder with .nca files)
  [optional]     title.keys (only if your copy is digital)

SPACE: about 13 GB free on the drive you install to (the exported game is ~12 GB).
Pick a folder you can write to (NOT inside "Program Files").

HOW IT WORKS
  1. This installer copies the launcher, suyu and the patches.
  2. The first time you open the launcher, the wizard appears:
       - legal notice (you must confirm the game is yours and you dumped it yourself),
       - you pick your files,
       - the launcher uses suyu to export the game into the
         "ASTRAL CHAIN - Dynarmic JIT" folder (with update 1.0.1 applied).
  3. If the automatic export fails, the wizard has a step-by-step manual guide.

Your original files are never modified or sent anywhere.
